# Published product list

This folder lists the **91 products** the customer app shows, and their barcodes.

- `catalogue-inventory.csv` — 91 products
- `product-barcodes.csv` — 164 product/barcode rows
- `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` — column meanings

The customer catalogue is these 91 products. Food 53, drinks 12, household 13,
personal care 9, other 4. Thirty-five are recipe-eligible.
